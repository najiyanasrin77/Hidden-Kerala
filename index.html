Beyond the Ordinary.

Kerala is more than its famous destinations. Discover hidden gems, experience breathtaking nature, and create unforgettable journeys with Hidden Kerala. Also prepare for your adventure with our smart trip and packing planner.

MUNNAR

Munnar is a beautiful hill station in Kerala, famous for its green tea plantations, misty mountains, cool climate, and peaceful scenery. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers and those looking for a relaxing escape.

KOCHI

Kochi is a vibrant coastal city known for its rich history, culture, beautiful streets, and famous attractions. With its blend of traditional and modern experiences, Kochi is a great destination for exploring Kerala's unique heritage.

ALAPPUZHA

Alappey is famous for its beautiful backwaters, houseboats, and peaceful waterways. Surrounded by coconut trees and scenic landscapes, it is a wonderful place to experience Kerala's natural beauty.
